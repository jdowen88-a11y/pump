## Updated Project Observations

2. Minimal priors are preferred: observe, measure, encode, reproduce, falsify before adding interpretation.
3. Observation and interpretation must be tagged separately.
4. The 21 Oceans remain unresolved.
5. Playground is the exploration namespace; current live convention is `Playground/...`.
6. Human ignition remains explicit: preserve the “do not turn key” principle.
7. Parallel streams should stay independently observable and retain provenance.
8. Blank-slate tests need initial conditions, seeds when available, transformations, outputs, and evaluation criteria.
9. Failure and unexpectedly minimal outputs are data.
10. Creative interpretation can remain expansive while executable behavior stays explicit, testable, reversible, and auditable.
